Igbo plant names

Sanko has mapped 4 Igbo plant names to accepted botanical names, each traceable to a published ethnobotanical survey.

  • Edo Terminalia avicennioides
  • Nwabuakonshi Nauclea latifolia
  • Okpo Atu Piliostigma reticulatum
  • Utakiri Vitex doniana

Sanko publishes plant name mappings and the parts reported in the source literature. It does not publish therapeutic claims, and nothing here is medical guidance.
