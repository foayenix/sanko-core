Yoruba plant name

Osekutu

Osekutu is a Yoruba plant name that published sources map to more than one species: Sida acuta and Sida linifolia. Sanko does not resolve it to a single taxon.

Sources disagree

Two or more published surveys record Osekutu against different species. Both readings are shown below with their sources. Sanko keeps the name unresolved rather than choosing between them.
