Yoruba plant name

Aworo-Ona

Aworo-Ona is a Yoruba plant name that published sources map to more than one species: Eleutheranthera ruderalis and Synedrella nodiflora. Sanko does not resolve it to a single taxon.

Sources disagree

Two or more published surveys record Aworo-Ona against different species. Both readings are shown below with their sources. Sanko keeps the name unresolved rather than choosing between them.
