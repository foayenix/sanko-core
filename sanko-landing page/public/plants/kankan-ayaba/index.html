Yoruba plant name

Kankan-ayaba

Kankan-ayaba is a Yoruba name for Luffa aegyptiaca, recorded in Ede South, Osun State, Nigeria and Alimosho, Badagry, Eti-Osa, Ijebu and Imota, Lagos State, Nigeria.
