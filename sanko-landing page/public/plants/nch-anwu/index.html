Igbo plant name

Nch-anwu

Nch-anwu is an Igbo plant name that published sources map to more than one species: Cymbopogon citratus and Thymus vulgaris. Sanko does not resolve it to a single taxon.

Sources disagree

Two or more published surveys record Nch-anwu against different species. Both readings are shown below with their sources. Sanko keeps the name unresolved rather than choosing between them.
