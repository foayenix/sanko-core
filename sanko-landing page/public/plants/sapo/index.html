Yoruba plant name

Sapo

Sapo is a Yoruba plant name that published sources map to more than one species: Anthocleista djalonensis and Anthocleista vogelii. Sanko does not resolve it to a single taxon.

Sources disagree

Two or more published surveys record Sapo against different species. Both readings are shown below with their sources. Sanko keeps the name unresolved rather than choosing between them.
