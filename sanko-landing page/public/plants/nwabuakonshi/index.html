Nwabuakonshi

Nwabuakonshi is a Igbo name for Nauclea latifolia, recorded in Barkin Ladi, Bassa, Bokkos, Jos North, Jos South, Kanam, Mangu, Pankshin, Qua'an Pan and Wase, Plateau State, Nigeria.
