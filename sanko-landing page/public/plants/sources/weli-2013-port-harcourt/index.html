Scope Sanko uses
All 84 displayed rows are included after adjudicating the two defects that held the table in staged review. Serials run 1-83 with no gap and no duplicate, and one further row carries no serial at all: the article's "83 species" counts the numbered rows, while Table 1 displays 84. The unnumbered row is Acanthus montanus, complete and taxonomically resolvable, so it is kept with serial_as_published null and its unnumbered_source_row flag rather than discarded. Three rows print the botanical name in the common-name column and the common name in the botanical column (serials 47, 48, 49 - Azadirachta indica, Moringa oleifera, Persea americana); GBIF matches the common-name cell to an accepted species and the botanical cell to nothing in all three, so the values are recorded under the attribute they describe and what each column printed is kept verbatim in source_columns_as_printed. Two species are listed twice by the authors (Capsicum annuum, Cymbopogon citratus) with differing vernacular spellings; both printings are kept. Seven rows carry a botanical name that does not resolve to an accepted species - misspellings such as Telfaria occidentalis and Dioscoreae cayennensis, and one genus-only 'Grewia sp' - and are left uncorrected, so the build withholds them as taxonomy_unresolved rather than this file guessing at what the authors meant. As with the other surveys, the per-row therapeutic indications are retained as published source text and are not exported.
