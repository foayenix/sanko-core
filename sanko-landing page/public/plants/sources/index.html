Provenance

Where these mappings come from

Every plant name Sanko publishes comes from one of 3 peer-reviewed ethnobotanical surveys of Nigerian flora.

  • Ethnobotanical Survey of Local Flora Used for Medicinal Purposes among Indigenous People in Five Areas in Lagos State, Nigeria

    CC BY 4.0 · 261 mappings published · 10.3390/plants11050633

  • Ethnobotanical survey of anti-asthmatic plants in South Western Nigeria

    open access; article-specific reuse terms not stated in the PDF · 37 mappings published · 10.4314/ajtcam.v5i4.31288

  • Ethnobotanical survey of medicinal plants used in the treatment of animal diarrhoea in Plateau State, Nigeria

    CC BY 2.0 · 73 mappings published · 10.1186/1746-6148-7-36

Registered but not published

Sanko holds further sources that do not meet its export conditions. They are listed here because an omission that nobody can see is not a provenance claim.

Handbook of African Medicinal Plants, Second Editionstaged_reviewOnly concise mapping facts and page locators are staged. Runtime export is blocked because most vernacular labels lack a row-specific country or locality and have not been reviewed by language practitioners.
Europe PMC and Crossref primary-source discoverydiscovery_onlyWritten by scripts/discover-sources.js. A ranked list of Nigerian ethnobotanical sources to acquire, and the resolution of the 79 original studies catalogued in anumudu-2025-nigeria-review to DOIs, licences and full texts. Candidates are citations, not data: no row here produces a vernacular mapping, and an acquired source only becomes ingestable when it is registered in this file in its own right.
Wikidata taxon labels in Nigerian languagesdiscovery_onlyWritten by scripts/import-wikidata-vernaculars.js. Labels are crowd-sourced and unsourced — no locality, no collector, no citation — and a large share of those returned in an African language are the botanical or English name filed in the wrong language. They are candidates for review, never mappings. A candidate reaches the runtime index only through practitioner confirmation, attributed to the reviewer who confirmed it and carrying the candidate source it came from.

Sanko publishes plant name mappings and the parts reported in the source literature. It does not publish therapeutic claims, and nothing here is medical guidance.
