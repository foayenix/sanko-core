Six of the 62 rows label their names Yoruba or Nupe; the other 56 state no language (interviews were conducted in both, and at least one unlabelled name, Karandafi, is Hausa). Unlabelled names keep a null language and are published as language not recorded rather than assigned one. June 12 is a nickname; it is kept as published text and is not a mapping.
