Where the study was conducted

Languages labelled in the source
Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Tiv and Efik
Scope Sanko uses
Survey of 700 herb sellers and practitioners (100 per state) in the North Central zone, 2018-2019; voucher numbers for most species. Table 2 labels every local name with its language: H Hausa, Y Yoruba, I Ibo, T Tiv per the footnote, and one cell (Efik). Excluded as published text: Aloe vera, printed as the Hausa name, and two unlabelled English entries (Sponge soap, Curry). Local names some rows print in the Common name column (Ukpo; Ora, Oha) carry no label and are not used. The pharmacology and toxicity review tables are not used. Not retracted (Europe PMC is-retracted: no); licence confirmed in Crossref.
