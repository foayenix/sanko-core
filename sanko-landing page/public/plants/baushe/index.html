Hausa plant name

Baushe

Baushe is a Hausa plant name that published sources map to more than one species: Combretum glutinosum, Terminalia avicennioides and Terminalia macroptera. Sanko does not resolve it to a single taxon.

Sources disagree

Two or more published surveys record Baushe against different species. Both readings are shown below with their sources. Sanko keeps the name unresolved rather than choosing between them.
