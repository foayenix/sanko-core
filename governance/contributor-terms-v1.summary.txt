The short version, in the words a practitioner is actually shown.

Kept deliberately to WhatsApp length: nobody reads three pages in a chat window,
and terms that were not read were not agreed to. It is hashed together with the
full text, so this summary cannot drift away from what it summarises without
producing a version nobody has accepted.

---
Your records stay yours. You can export them or delete them whenever you want,
and nothing leaves Sanko's own machines without asking you first.

Sanko may use your corrections to improve how well it understands you, and count
your records in totals with no names, plants or places in them.

Anything beyond that — research, licensing, publication, a dataset — is a
separate question each time. You are told who is asking and what for, you can
say no and keep using Sanko, and what you get in return is written down.
