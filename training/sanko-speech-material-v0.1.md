# Sanko Speech material inventory v0.1

Status: draft for practitioner and native-speaker review. This is a speech-recognition
recording brief, not medical advice or a list of clinically validated treatments.

## How to use this inventory

Record each approved item in isolation and in natural sentences. Preserve the speaker's
own pronunciation, dialect, wording, and code-switching. Do not coach every speaker toward
one "standard" accent. Ask the reviewer to provide the preferred spelling after recording.

For every clip, capture speaker consent, language(s), dialect/region, recording device,
noise setting, verbatim transcript, reviewer, and approval status. Keep unreviewed clips
out of the gold training and evaluation sets.

The local names below are candidates drawn from Sanko's existing plant and evaluation
data. Botanical taxonomy checks do not verify pronunciation, tone marks, translation, or
community usage. A practitioner or relevant language reviewer must approve each term.

## 6. Common practitioner intents

### Starting and describing a record

- I want to record a formulation.
- Let me tell you how I prepare it.
- This is the plant I use.
- I know it by this local name.
- In my area we call it something different.
- I use the leaves, not the root.
- I combine these two plants.
- This is for external use only.
- I did not specify a quantity.
- I do not know the botanical name.
- I learned this from my parent, elder, teacher, or community.

### Clarifying uncertainty

- I am not sure of the spelling.
- That is how we pronounce it here.
- The same name can mean another plant.
- Do not guess which plant I mean.
- Show me the possible matches.
- None of those matches is correct.
- I need to send a photograph.
- The recording was not clear; let me repeat it.
- Ask me again about the amount.
- Leave that field empty.

### Correcting Sanko

- That transcript is correct.
- That word is wrong.
- You wrote the wrong plant name.
- Replace this word with this one.
- I said leaves, not seeds.
- I said boil, not soak.
- I said twice, not three times.
- The amount is wrong.
- The duration is wrong.
- Remove the plant you added.
- Do not add anything I did not say.
- Keep my original wording.
- Now it is correct.

### Record actions

- Save this formulation.
- Save it as a draft.
- Do not save it yet.
- Read it back to me.
- Show me what you recorded.
- Update the preparation.
- Add the missing quantity.
- Remove that entry.
- Find my last formulation.
- This is a different formulation.
- I am finished.

## 8. Sentence-pattern matrix

Have reviewers create natural utterances by combining the slots below. Do not generate
every mathematical combination; select phrases people would genuinely say.

1. `[plant name] + [plant part]`
2. `[quantity] + [plant name] + [plant part]`
3. `[preparation action] + [quantity] + [plant material]`
4. `[plant name] + [preparation method] + [duration]`
5. `[amount] + [frequency] + [number of days]`
6. `[local name] + also called + [alias]`
7. `[incorrect recognition] + correction + [correct term]`
8. `[known plant] + [unresolved plant] + request for clarification`
9. `[instruction] + negative contrast: not X, but Y`
10. `[complete description] + save/draft/do-not-save intent`

## 9. Recording variation checklist

For a useful subset of the material, capture:

- quiet room and ordinary outdoor/background noise;
- inexpensive Android phone and higher-quality phone;
- close microphone and phone held at natural voice-note distance;
- slow, ordinary, and fast delivery;
- younger and older adult speakers;
- different genders and relevant regions;
- single language and genuine code-switching;
- isolated words, short phrases, and full conversational turns;
- repetition, hesitation, correction, and interruption;
- the same name inside several different sentences.

Do not manufacture demographic balance by relabelling people. Track actual coverage and
report gaps honestly.

## 10. Required review before recording at scale

- Practitioner confirms that each phrase is natural and relevant.
- Native-language reviewer confirms spelling, tone marks, and transcript conventions.
- Plant reviewer flags ambiguous or disputed names without forcing a botanical match.
- Governance reviewer confirms the recording and model-training consent language.
- Dataset owner assigns a version and freezes the evaluation subset before training.
