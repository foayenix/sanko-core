# Sanko Brand Identity

Status: active identity system · version 1.0 · 14 August 2026

This guide governs Sanko’s public website, product shell, institutional communications, reports, presentations and partnership material. It complements `DESIGN.md`: this document owns brand meaning, identity architecture and asset use; `DESIGN.md` owns interface execution.

## 1. Brand foundation

### Purpose

Sanko turns living traditional-medicine knowledge into private, structured and attributable records without requiring practitioners to surrender control of their knowledge.

### Positioning

**Evidence infrastructure for African traditional medicine.**

Sanko combines a familiar practitioner interface—WhatsApp—with locally operated AI, medicinal-plant name mapping, practitioner-scoped records, patient tracking and Nagoya-aligned provenance. It is documentation infrastructure, not a clinical authority, certification body or owner of traditional knowledge.

### Brand promise

**Turn living knowledge into verifiable evidence—while keeping its source visible and its ownership intact.**

### Brand character

- **Source-first:** knowledge begins with a named practitioner and remains attributable.
- **Precise:** interfaces and language are inspectable, measured and explicit.
- **Sovereign:** privacy, local operation and practitioner control are structural values.
- **Institution-ready:** credible to networks, researchers, funders and regulators without imitating clinical authority.
- **Culturally grounded:** context comes from ownership, language and provenance—not decorative stereotypes.

## 2. Identity architecture

Sanko uses one active identity system.

| Element | Status | Role |
|---|---|---|
| D1 Source Mark | Primary | The recognisable source and product symbol |
| D1 + SANKO horizontal lockup | Primary | Default website, product and institutional signature |
| SANKO wordmark | Secondary | Space-constrained contexts where the symbol is already present nearby |
| Knowledge thread | Signature graphic | Shows capture, transformation, provenance and governed movement |
| Woven knot | Supporting system motif | Marks meaningful joins or transfer points, never a second logo |
| Legacy bird | Archival | Optional About/Origins or time-bounded migration material only |
| Legacy S monogram | Retired | No active use |
| Legacy seal | Retired | Never a trust, evidence, compliance or quality mark |

**The One Identity Rule.** Never present D1, the bird, the monogram, the seal or the woven knot as peer logo choices. D1 is the identity; the thread and knot belong to the visual language.

## 3. Logo system

### The D1 Source Mark

The mark is a compact block silhouette with a chamfered top-right corner, one square counter and one right arm.

- The **body** represents the original knowledge source.
- The **counter** represents an identifiable, traceable record.
- The **chamfer** signals a deliberate transition rather than passive storage.
- The **right arm** moves knowledge into a structured system and may initiate a provenance thread in diagrams.

This meaning should be demonstrated through repeated use and the knowledge-thread system; do not add literal leaves, birds, documents or medical symbols to explain it.

### Preferred lockup

Use the horizontal D1 + SANKO lockup wherever space permits. The wordmark is uppercase Archivo at weight 850 with restrained positive tracking. Do not typeset substitute lockups in unrelated fonts.

### Colour variants

- **On dark:** Mineral White mark and wordmark on Brand Indigo, Field Indigo or Deep Indigo.
- **On light:** Brand Indigo mark and wordmark on Mineral White.
- Do not set the primary logo in Medicinal Chartreuse. Chartreuse signals action and live information; its rarity is part of the system.
- Do not place the logo on photographs, gradients, patterns or colours outside the approved palette without a solid approved holding field.

### Clear space

Let **x** equal the width of the square counter in the D1 mark. Keep at least **1x** clear around the mark and **1x** around the complete lockup. No rule, thread, text, edge or partner mark may enter this area.

### Minimum size

- Mark only: 24px digital or 8mm print.
- Horizontal lockup: 140px wide digital or 35mm print.
- Below these sizes, use the wordmark at a legible size rather than compressing the complete lockup.

The mark should still be tested at 16px for favicon contexts, but 16px is not the general minimum for brand applications.

### Misuse

Do not:

- redraw, round, rotate, stretch or add strokes to the Source Mark;
- close the counter or shorten the right arm;
- merge D1 with the woven knot, bird, leaf, sun or seal;
- put the legacy bird inside D1;
- apply gradients, shadows, texture, bevels or animation to the logo;
- use the seal to imply review, certification, validation or approval;
- use the mark without SANKO in unfamiliar public contexts unless recognition has already been established.

**The Signal Rarity Rule.** Chartreuse identifies action, transformation or an important live quantity. It is not ambient decoration and never becomes a general page wash.

**The Two-Indigo Rule.** Brand Indigo owns the identity. Field Indigo owns large digital environments. Do not introduce additional near-identical indigos.

## 5. Typography

### Primary family

**Archivo Variable** is the brand voice across display, headings, navigation and body text. It is broad, declarative and institutional without becoming clinical.

- Display: weight 820; compact line-height; tracking no tighter than `-0.04em`.
- Headlines: weight 750–820.
- Titles and navigation: weight 650–750.
- Body: weight 400–500; line-height 1.45–1.6; maximum measure approximately 65 characters.
- Wordmark: uppercase; weight 850; tracking `0.025em`.

### Data family

**IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for data, provenance, plant names, measurements, record metadata and short technical labels. It is not a general display costume.

**The Plain-Language Lead Rule.** Lead with the practitioner or institutional outcome. Name technical implementation only after the value is clear.

## 6. Signature visual language

### Knowledge thread

The angular chartreuse thread connects meaningful transformations: source capture, mapping, formulation, patient follow-up, consent and governance. It may begin at the D1 right arm when the relationship is explicit.

- Use square ports and 45-degree knots at real transfer points.
- Keep paths orthogonal or deliberately angular.
- Animate once from an already visible base state; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Never use the thread as an empty border or decorative maze.

### Records and evidence panels

Records are flat, rectangular and inspectable. One-pixel rules, square registration marks and data labels create depth without shadows.

- Clearly label illustrative records.
- Keep provenance and source attribution visible.
- Never style an illustrative panel as clinical proof or regulatory approval.

### Botanical imagery

Use botanically grounded linework, sourced documentary material or no image. Avoid generic wellness photography, anonymous “African” patterns, decorative leaves and mystical AI imagery.

## 7. Voice and messaging

### Voice

Sanko sounds calm, exact and accountable. It explains complex infrastructure in plain institutional language while keeping practitioners present as people with rights—not as data sources.

Write with:

- short declarative sentences;
- active verbs and concrete subjects;
- explicit source, consent and ownership language;
- qualified governance language where evidence is incomplete;
- human outcomes before implementation details.

### Messaging pillars

1. **Familiar capture:** practitioners use WhatsApp voice, text and photographs.
2. **Private structure:** knowledge becomes practitioner-scoped formulation and patient records.
3. **Visible provenance:** attribution follows knowledge from source to later use.
4. **Responsible access:** research and institutional use require consent and appropriate benefit-sharing processes.

### Preferred language

- “Nagoya-aligned,” not “Nagoya certified” or “fully compliant.”
- “Structured records,” not “clinically validated evidence.”
- “Patient tracking,” not “proven patient outcomes.”
- “Practitioner-owned knowledge,” not “Sanko’s traditional-knowledge database.”
- “Evidence infrastructure,” not “AI-powered” or “Responsible AI,” as the primary value proposition—responsibility is demonstrated through governance, consent and local operation, never asserted as a descriptor.

## 8. Applications

### Website and product

- Use the horizontal on-dark lockup in indigo navigation.
- Use mark-only only when Sanko is already named within the same product shell.
- Keep the WhatsApp action chartreuse; the logo remains Mineral White or Brand Indigo.

### Institutional documents

- Use the on-light lockup on Mineral White covers and pages.
- Use the on-dark lockup on Brand Indigo cover bands or section dividers.
- Do not place the legacy seal on evidence reports, governance documents or partnership agreements.

### Partnerships

- Separate partner marks with at least 2x clear space.
- Match optical height, not bounding-box height.
- Use a neutral one-colour partner-logo row where possible; never recolour partner marks into chartreuse.

## 9. Governance

- The approved active identity is D1 Source Mark + SANKO.
- New identity assets must preserve the exact D1 geometry unless a documented recognition test approves a revision.
- Legacy files remain available only for archival traceability and must not be reintroduced by convenience.
- Every new brand asset needs light/dark validation, small-size inspection and an accessible name where it carries meaning.
- Changes to logo geometry, primary palette or wordmark typography require an explicit brand-version change.
